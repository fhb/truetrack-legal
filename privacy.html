TrueTrack – Privacy Policy

App: TrueTrack: Flight Planner (iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch) · Last updated: 8 November 2026

EnglishDeutsch

In short

  • TrueTrack has no user accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your trips, pilot profile, logbook, aircraft and documents stay on your devices and in your own iCloud. We (the developer) cannot read them.
  • We only receive data when you send a problem report — and you see what is included before you send it.

2. Data that stays with you

Trips, routes, notes, your pilot profile (licences, ratings, medical, currency), logbook entries, aircraft, crew and passengers, documents and photos are stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync in TrueTrack, Apple stores them in your private iCloud so your devices stay in sync. Only you (and Apple, under Apple's privacy policy) have access; the developer has none. Backups you make in the app stay on your device or wherever you save them.

Contacts, calendar, photos, camera, microphone, speech recognition and location are used only on your device and only after you allow them in iOS. Location is used to show your position on the map, for directions to the airfield (Apple Maps) and for arrival reminders; it is not sent to us or to third parties by TrueTrack.

3. Sharing with people you choose

If you share a trip or your live pilot card, Apple's iCloud sharing makes that data available to the people you invite. You can stop sharing at any time in the app.

4. Problem reports (the only data we receive)

When you send a report under Settings › Help & Feedback, the app sends it to us through Apple's CloudKit (Apple acts as our processor). A report contains:

  • your text and the category you chose; optionally your contact details if you enter them;
  • app version, build, device model, iOS version, the open screen, language and time zone;
  • attachments you leave switched on: screenshots, the trip that is open (it can contain names and contact details of crew and passengers), the app's diagnostic log and crash/hang reports;
  • an anonymous iCloud user identifier that is specific to TrueTrack (it does not reveal your Apple ID or e-mail), so you can see replies in "My Reports".

Purpose: to answer you and to find and fix the problem. Legal basis: your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in a working app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Retention: 12 months after the case is closed; earlier deletion on request at any time.

5. Services the app contacts to get data

To show weather, maps and airfield information, the app requests data from the services below. These requests contain what is needed to answer them (for example an airfield code, map area or coordinates) and, technically, your IP address. They do not contain your name or your trips.

6. Assistant (optional)

The assistant only works with an API key you enter (Anthropic Claude or Google Gemini). Your questions and the trip details needed to answer them go directly from your device to that provider under your own account and its terms. Before data goes to Gemini, names, phone numbers, e-mail addresses and weights of other people are removed. The developer does not receive these requests.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and to complain to a data protection supervisory authority. To delete a report or ask about your data, write to the e-mail address above.

8. Children

TrueTrack is meant for pilots and is not directed at children.

9. Changes

We update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows the current version.

2. Daten, die bei dir bleiben

Reisen, Routen, Notizen, dein Pilotenprofil (Lizenzen, Berechtigungen, Medical, Übung), Flugbucheinträge, Flugzeuge, Crew und Passagiere, Dokumente und Fotos werden auf deinem Gerät gespeichert. Zugriff hast nur du (und Apple gemäß der Datenschutzrichtlinie von Apple); der Entwickler hat keinen. Backups aus der App bleiben auf deinem Gerät oder dort, wo du sie speicherst.

Kontakte, Kalender, Fotos, Kamera, Mikrofon, Spracherkennung und Standort werden nur auf deinem Gerät und nur nach deiner Freigabe in iOS genutzt. Der Standort dient der Anzeige auf der Karte, der Wegbeschreibung zum Flugplatz (Apple Karten) und Ankunftserinnerungen; TrueTrack sendet ihn weder an uns noch an Dritte.

4. Problemberichte (die einzigen Daten, die wir erhalten)

Wenn du unter Einstellungen › Hilfe & Feedback einen Bericht sendest, übermittelt die App ihn über Apples CloudKit an uns (Apple ist dabei unser Auftragsverarbeiter). Ein Bericht enthält:

  • deinen Text und die gewählte Kategorie; optional deine Kontaktdaten, wenn du sie einträgst;
  • App-Version, Build, Gerätemodell, iOS-Version, den geöffneten Bildschirm, Sprache und Zeitzone;
  • Anhänge, die du eingeschaltet lässt: Bildschirmfotos, die geöffnete Reise (sie kann Namen und Kontaktdaten von Crew und Passagieren enthalten), das Diagnoseprotokoll der App sowie Absturz- und Hänger-Berichte;
  • eine anonyme, nur für TrueTrack gültige iCloud-Benutzerkennung (sie verrät weder deine Apple-ID noch deine E-Mail), damit du Antworten unter „Meine Berichte" siehst.

Zweck: dir antworten und das Problem finden und beheben. Rechtsgrundlage: deine Anfrage (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO) und unser berechtigtes Interesse an einer funktionierenden App (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO). Speicherdauer: 12 Monate nach Abschluss des Falls; auf Wunsch jederzeit früher gelöscht.
