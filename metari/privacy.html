METARI – Privacy Policy

App: METARI (iPhone, Apple Watch, widgets) · Last updated: 8 November 2026

EnglishDeutsch

In short

  • METARI has no user accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your favourites, settings and trainer progress stay on your device.
  • We (the developer) receive no personal data from the app.

2. Data on your device

Favourite stations, settings and trainer results are stored on your device (shared with the METARI widgets and the Apple Watch app on the same device). They are not sent to us.

Location (only if you allow it in iOS) is used on your device to find the stations nearest to you. It is not sent to us or to third parties by METARI.

Weather interpretation with Apple Intelligence runs on your device using Apple's on-device model; the reports are not sent to us.

3. Weather data

To show METAR, TAF and forecasts, the app requests them from the Aviation Weather Center (NOAA, USA) and MET Norway. These requests contain station codes or coordinates of the stations and, technically, your IP address; they contain no name or account.

4. Purchases (METARI Pro)

Subscriptions and the free trial are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details; Apple only tells the app whether Pro is active.

5. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR) and to complain to a supervisory authority. Since we hold no personal data from the app, there is usually nothing to disclose; write to the e-mail address above with any question.

6. Changes

We update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows the current version.

2. Daten auf deinem Gerät

Favorisierte Stationen, Einstellungen und Trainer-Ergebnisse werden auf deinem Gerät gespeichert (gemeinsam mit den METARI-Widgets und der Apple-Watch-App auf demselben Gerät). Sie werden nicht an uns übermittelt.

Der Standort (nur nach deiner Freigabe in iOS) dient auf deinem Gerät dazu, die nächstgelegenen Stationen zu finden. METARI sendet ihn weder an uns noch an Dritte.

Die Wetterinterpretation mit Apple Intelligence läuft mit Apples Modell auf deinem Gerät; die Meldungen werden nicht an uns gesendet.

3. Wetterdaten

Für METAR, TAF und Vorhersagen fragt die App das Aviation Weather Center (NOAA, USA) und MET Norway ab. Die Anfragen enthalten Stationskennungen bzw. Koordinaten der Stationen und technisch bedingt deine IP-Adresse – keinen Namen und kein Konto.
