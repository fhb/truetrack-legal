Plan the whole trip.
Every day, every leg.

TrueTrack puts a multi-day VFR trip on one calendar: legs, daylight, opening hours and PPR, fuel, weather and the checks that matter – before you go and on the day.

Coming to the App Store Join the beta

Built around the way you fly

From the first idea to the last landing of the trip: one place for the plan, the limits and the paperwork.

◷

Days on a calendar

Every leg in UTC with local time beside it, ground times, nights away and how each day ends.

☀︎

Daylight and hours

Civil dawn and dusk, airfield opening hours, PPR windows and closings – shown where they limit your day.

⛽︎

Fuel and aircraft

Fuel per leg and reserve, your aircraft profiles and costs, split fairly between pilot and passengers.

☁︎

Weather, decoded

METAR and TAF with every group explained, flight categories on the map and forecasts along the route.

✓

Readiness and risk

Currency from your logbook, personal minimums and a flight risk check with what each answer means.

⌚︎

On the day

Widgets, Live Activity and the Apple Watch: next off-block, daylight left and the airfields of the day.

A planning aid, not a navigation instrument and not an official briefing. Always check current official sources before flight.
